
# The Book of Life

**Genre:** Animation, Adventure, Fantasy  
**Directed by:** Jorge R. Gutierrez  

##  Overview
**The Book of Life** is an animated film that celebrates *Mexican culture* and the *Day of the Dead* tradition.  
It tells the story of *Manolo Sánchez*, a young man torn between fulfilling his family's expectations and following his heart to become a musician.

## Main Characters
- **Manolo Sánchez** – A kind-hearted musician and bullfighter who loves Maria.  
- **María Posada** – Brave and compassionate, especially with animals, she stands for what she believes in.  
- **Joaquín Mondragon** – A heroic soldier and Maria and Manolo’s childhood friend.  
- **La Muerte** – The ruler of the Land of the Remembered.  
- **Xibalba** – The Ruler of the Land of the Forgotten.
